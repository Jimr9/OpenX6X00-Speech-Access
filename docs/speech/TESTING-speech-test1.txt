OpenX6X00 speech, test 1: what to press and what you should hear

Key names are the ones printed on the radio, the same keys as in our own build.
Do every test from the main screen, with nothing open. If something is open, press ESC, or press the VOL knob, until you are back.
After each test, note one of three results: OK, silent, or wrong (and what it said).


PART 1: THE KNOBS

1. Main tuning knob (the big one)
Turn it a few steps, then stop.
Expect: about one second after you stop, the frequency, for example "14.074".
working

2. VOL knob, turned
Turn it one step.
Expect: "Volume, 12" (your level). Turning on says only the number after a few steps; every few announcements the name comes back.

3. VOL knob, pressed
Press it once.
Expect: "Volume, 12" again. VOL is now in choosing mode. Turn it one step.
Expect: the name and value of another control, for example "Squelch, 0". Each step names the next one.
Press VOL again to go back to changing the value.

4. MFK knob (the small knob), turned
Turn it one step.
Expect: the name and value of the control it is on, for example "Filter low, 100". Every few announcements the name comes back.

5. MFK knob, pressed
Press it once, then turn it one step.
Expect: the name of the next control and its value. Each step names the next one.

PART 2: THE KEYS ON THE TOP OF THE RADIO

6. SSB key
Tap: switches between L S B and U S B, and says which.
Hold: digital on or off, for example "U S B digital".
working

7. CW key
Tap: "C W", or "C W reverse" on the next tap.
working

8. AM key
Tap: "A M", or "N F M" on the next tap.
working

9. BAND up, BAND down
Tap: the band, the frequency and the mode, for example "20 meters, 14.074, U S B".
working

10. PRE key
Tap: "Pre on" or "Pre off".
Hold: "Att on" or "Att off" (the attenuator).
working

11. AGC key
Tap: the next AGC setting, for example "AGC Fast".
Hold: split on or off, it should say "Split" with the VFO.
working

12. ATU key
Tap: "ATU on" or "ATU off".
Hold: starts a tuning cycle. It transmits, so do it only with an antenna on. Nothing may be said here; tell me.
working

13. A/B key
Tap: the VFO and its frequency, for example "VFO B, 14.074".
working

14. F/S key
Tap: the tuning step, for example "Freq step, 100 Hz".
working

15. V/M key
Tap: the F-keys go to the memory page. Expect its page name.

16. LOCK key
Tap: "Dial locked". Tap again: "Dial unlocked".
Do not hold it: held for several seconds it restarts the radio's base board.
working


PART 3: THE KEYS ON THE FRONT

17. GEN key
Tap: the F-keys change to the next GEN page, and it says the page, for example "GEN page 2".
Each tap steps to the next GEN page.

18. F1 to F5 (below the screen)
These are not the same buttons as in our build, so what they say will differ from what you know. That is expected.
Press GEN until you hear "GEN page 1". Then press F1, F2, F3, F4 and F5 one at a time, and write down what each one says.
Expect: either what the button did (for example "Spectrum ...") or the button's name. A greyed out button says its name and "unavailable".
Tell me any that say nothing, or say something you cannot make sense of.

19. KEY, DFN, DFL keys
Tap: the F-keys change to that key's page, and it says the page name.
Then try F1 to F5 on one of those pages, as in 18.

20. APP key
Tap: the APPS screen opens, a grid of apps.
Known problem: right now it reads a long list of all of them at once. I will fix that.
Turn the MFK: it should name each app as you move. Press APP or ESC to close it without opening anything.

21. MSG key
In SSB: opens the voice messages window. In CW: opens the CW messages window.
Expect its name, then what the knob is on. Press ESC to close it.

22. Power key
Tap: turns the screen off or on. Nothing is said. That is expected.
Hold: the radio shuts down. It says "Power off".


PART 4: SETTINGS

23. Open Settings
Settings is on one of the GEN pages. Use the F-key that says "Settings" in test 18.
Expect: "Settings", then the first row.
Turn the MFK: each row's name and its value.
Press the MFK on a row to change it, turn to choose, press again to keep it.
Press ESC to go back.

24. The Voice tab
In Settings, page 3 of the tabs has "Voice". It holds the voice mode, which voice, rate, pitch, volume, and the delays.
Expect: each one read with its value, and changes read as you make them.


PART 5: TRANSMIT

25. PTT, with a dummy load or an antenna
Hold PTT while something is being said.
Expect: the speech stops at once, and nothing is said while transmitting.
Let go: receive audio and speech come back.
