OpenX6X00 speech, test 3

One sheet for everything in this build. Go through what you can, in any order, and write after each test: OK, silent, or wrong (and what it said).
If something is silent or wrong, do not power off: the radio keeps a speech log in memory that I can read once it is on WiFi (part B).


HOW TO GET AROUND IN THIS BUILD

- Small knob (MFK): moves through rows, lists, keys and menus. Press it to choose.
- VOL knob: turning sets the volume. Pressing it is your ESC (new in this build): it backs out of a menu or a list, and closes the window you are in. On the main screen, pressing it switches VOL between changing the volume and choosing what VOL controls.
- GEN, APP, KEY, DFN, DFL: each one closes the window you are in and shows its F-key pages. Each tap steps to its next page.
- The page names you will hear:
  - GEN: "VOL page 1", "VOL page 2", then "MFK page 1" to "MFK page 4".
  - APP: "APP page 1" to "APP page 6".
  - KEY: "KEY page 1", "KEY page 2", then "CW page 1", "CW page 2".
  - DFN: "DFN page 1" to "DFN page 3".
  - DFL: "Filter page".
  - V/M: "MEM page 1", "MEM page 2".
- When a window closes you hear "Closed", then the F-key page that comes back.
- Power key, tapped: reads the screen (where the radio is, or the open window's text). Held: turns the radio off.


PART A: THE MAIN SCREEN

A1. Power on
Expect: "Open X 6 X hundred ready". Later, once WiFi has joined a network: "WiFi connected, address 192 dot 168 dot ...".
On a freshly flashed card the first-start guide comes up first: see part C.

A2. Screen saver wake
Leave the radio alone for 5 minutes. Then turn the VOL knob one step.
Expect: "Volume, 12" (your level). The first turn is no longer swallowed.

A3. VOL knob pressed
Press it once.
Expect: "VOL, choose, Volume, 12". Turn it one step.
Expect: another control's name and value, for example "RF gain, 100". Each step names the next.
Press it again.
Expect: "VOL, change, RF gain, 100" (whichever control you stopped on). Turning now changes that control.
Choose Volume again before you leave it, or VOL keeps changing the other control.

A4. Small knob (MFK) pressed
Press it once.
Expect: "M F K, choose, Zoom, x1" (or whatever it is on). Turn it: each control by name and value.
Press it again.
Expect: "M F K, change, ..." and turning changes that control.

A5. DFL key
Tap.
Expect: "Filter page". F1 to F4: filter low, filter high, bandwidth, step.

A6. DFN key, then F5
Tap DFN until "DFN page 1". Press F5.
Expect: "DNF auto, On" or "Off". (Silent in test 2. If still silent, tell me.)


PART B: WIFI (so the radio is on your network)

B1. Open Settings
Tap APP, turn the small knob to "Settings, All settings, 23 of 23" and press it.
Or: tap APP until "APP page 2", then F2.
Expect: "Settings", then the first row.

B2. The Wireless tab
In Settings, turn the small knob to the tabs on the left and find "Wireless". Press it, then turn to the network rows.
Expect, for each network: its name, connected or not, saved or not, secured or open, the signal in percent, and its place, for example "HomeNet, secured, signal 70 percent, 1 of 4".

B3. Join your network
Press the small knob on your network.
Expect: a menu, for example "HomeNet, menu, Connect, 1 of 2". Turn: each choice. Press on Connect.
For a secured network a password window opens.
Expect: "Password for HomeNet. Small knob: the keys, press: type, done: finish", then the keyboard and the key the knob is on.

B4. Typing on the on-screen keyboard
Turn the small knob: each key is said, for example "cap Q", "W", "backspace", "space".
Capitals are said with "cap". The key "A B C" or "a b c" switches between upper and lower case, "1 #" to numbers and symbols.
Symbols are said by name: "dot", "dash", "underscore", "at" and so on.
Press the small knob to type the key: the key is said again as it is typed.
When done, find "done" (the tick) and press it.
Expect, a few seconds later: "WiFi connected, address 192 dot 168 dot ...". Write the address down for me.

B5. Leaving a menu or the keyboard
In the menu or on the keyboard, press VOL.
Expect: it closes without doing anything.


PART C: THE FIRST-START GUIDE

You only see this on a freshly flashed card, or from Settings > General > STATION > First-start guide.

C1. Each step
Expect, at each step: "First start, step 1 of 5, Language", its explanation, which F-keys do what ("F2 language, F4 skip, F5 next"), then the choice the knob is on.
F1 back (from step 2), F2 the step's own action, F3 locator (the callsign step only), F4 skip, F5 next.

C2. Skipping it
Press VOL twice quickly.
Expect: the guide closes.


PART D: APPS AND MODES

D1. APP key: the apps grid
Expect: "Apps, Modes, F T 8, J S 8, P S K, S S T V, 1 of 23". Turning: each app. Press: opens it. VOL: closes it.

D2. Modes
In the apps grid, press the small knob on Modes.
Expect: "Digital modes, R T T Y, 45 bd, 1 of 14". Turning: each mode.

D3. FT8
In Digital modes, turn to FT8 and press.
Expect: "F T 8". Then, while it receives:
- After each 15-second period, a short count: "3 C Q, 5 other", or "4 C Q".
- A message for you, at once: "For you: W 1 X Y Z, K 1 A B C, minus 10".
Turn the small knob through the list.
Expect: each row with callsigns and grids spelled out, the country, for a CQ whether you worked them ("new", "worked"), and its place: "C Q, K 1 A B C, F N 4 2, United States, new, 7 of 12".
Press VOL or GEN to close FT8.

D4. MSG key (in SSB): the voice keyer
Expect: "Voice keyer. Small knob: the messages, press: listen. F2 record, F3 listen, F4 send, F5 auto call", then the first message, or "No messages yet".
Turn the small knob: each message, its length and place: "CQ, 4.5 seconds, 1 of 3".
Listening: "Listening", then "Ready" when done.
Recording: nothing is said while it records, so your recording stays clean.
Press VOL to close it.

D5. MSG key (in CW mode): CW messages
Expect: "CW messages. Small knob: the messages. F2 send, F3 new, F4 edit", then the first message with callsigns spelled out: "C Q, C Q, D E, K 1 A B C, 1 of 4".
Turn the small knob: each message. Press VOL to close it.

D6. Channels (memories)
Open Channels from the apps grid (number 6), or with V/M on the hand mic.
Turn the small knob: each channel, "40 meters net, 7.150, L S B", or "7.074, U S B" when it has no name.
With no channels: "No channels. F2 adds the current frequency".
Press the small knob to tune a channel (the window closes).

D7. SWR scan (transmits: antenna or dummy load on)
Open SWR Scan from the apps grid (number 8). F1 is Run and Stop, F2 Scale, F3 Span.
F3: the span, "Span, 100 kHz".
Press F1 to start: nothing is said while it scans, because it transmits a carrier.
Press F1 again to stop.
Expect: "Stopped, lowest S W R 1.3 at 14.150, highest 2.8 at 14.350".

D8. Decoded text read aloud (CW decoder, RTTY, PSK31, PSK63, Olivia, JTTY, NavTex)
What these decoders receive is read aloud in bursts, at each pause or line end, with callsigns spelled out.
A burst waits for the one before it to finish. A knob or key still cuts it short.
Try CW with the decoder on, or PSK31 from Digital modes, on a busy frequency.
Expect: the decoded text read, for example "C Q, C Q, D E, K 1 A B C, K".
On a noisy band the decoders print rubbish and it gets read too: turn it off in Settings > Voice > VOICE BEHAVIOUR > "Read decoded text".

D9. JS8
From Digital modes. Expect: a message for you at once, "For you, from K 1 A B C: ..."; other messages read in turn, "From K 1 A B C: ...". Same switch as D8.

D10. Scanner
From the apps grid (number 7). F3 starts, pauses and resumes it; F2 chooses what it scans; F4 skips. It needs channels to scan.
Expect: "Scanning", then on a busy channel "Busy, 40 meters net, 7.150, L S B"; "Paused, ..." and "Scanner stopped" as they happen.

D11. DX Cluster
From the apps grid (number 2). Needs WiFi.
Expect: "DX cluster. Small knob: the spots, press: tune". Turning: each spot, "K 1 A B C, 14.025, up 2, spotted by W 1 A W, 3 of 40". Press the small knob to tune it.

D12. Recorder
From the apps grid (number 9). F1 Rec: anything still being said stops, and nothing is said while it records. Stopping: "RX recorder is off". The file list reads each file as you turn.

D13. SSTV
From Digital modes. Expect: "Receiving picture, Martin 1" (or the mode) when a picture starts, then "SSTV picture ... saved", or "Decoding failed".

D14. NavTex DSC calls
NavTex in its DSC mode: each call received is said, "D S C call, ALL SHIPS, ...".

D15. Read the screen: the power key
Tap the power key (do not hold it).
On the main screen, expect where the radio is: "14.074, U S B, VFO A, 20 meters, 10:42, battery 80 percent".
In any window, expect all of that window's text read top to bottom. Use it in the apps that are not wired yet.
With speech on, the tap no longer turns the screen off. Holding it still turns the radio off.

D16. Other apps
Any other app: you hear its name, and a short description of where the knob is. Their own screens are not wired for speech yet. Tell me which ones you want next.


PART E: THE HAND MIC (if you use one)

E1. Number keys
Tap a number: the band printed on it. Expect: "20 meters, 14.074, U S B".
Hold a number (1 to 9): that memory. Expect: "Loaded from memory 3".

E2. MODE
Tap: the next mode. Expect: "L S B", "U S B", "C W" and so on.

E3. XFC
Tap: the other VFO. Expect: "VFO B, 14.074".

E4. TUNER
Tap: "ATU on" or "ATU off".

E5. UP and DOWN
Tap: the next or previous channel. Expect: "Channel 40 meters net, 7.150", or "No channels".

E6. A/B on the radio, held
Expect: "VFO A copied to B".


PART F: TRANSMIT

F1. PTT, with a dummy load or an antenna
Hold PTT while something is being said.
Expect: the speech stops at once, nothing is said while transmitting. Let go: receive audio and speech come back.


WHAT IS NEW IN TEST 3, FOR REFERENCE
- VOL press is ESC in windows; "Closed" when a window closes.
- VOL and small-knob presses say "choose" or "change", with the control's name.
- DFL says "Filter page".
- The IP address is said when WiFi connects.
- The WiFi window and Settings > Wireless: networks with signal, saved, secured; the action menu; the password.
- The on-screen keyboard: each key as you move, each key as you type.
- The first-start guide reads each step.
- FT8: the list, messages for you, a count after each period.
- The voice keyer (MSG): its messages and states, and silence while recording.
- CW messages (MSG in CW) and Channels: each entry read.
- Any list: the first knob step reads the first row (it was silent).
- Hand mic: band keys, MODE, XFC, TUNER, UP and DOWN; A/B held.
- SWR scan: the result after Stop; nothing is said during the scan or an ATU tuning cycle.
- Decoded text read aloud (CW, RTTY, PSK, Olivia, JTTY, NavTex), with a switch in Settings > Voice.
- JS8 messages, the Scanner, the DX Cluster, the Recorder, SSTV, NavTex DSC calls.
- The power key's tap reads the screen.
- Windows not wired yet: their title only, not every label on them.
