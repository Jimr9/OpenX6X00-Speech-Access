OpenX6X00 speech, test 6

Everything from test 5 is in this build (TESTING-speech-test5.txt still applies), plus the fixes below.
Write after each test: OK, silent, or wrong (and what it said).
Remember: an ATU tune or SWR sweep can stop speech (the base board's fault); power off and on to get it back.


1. Weather, line by line (APPS number 22)
Expect when it opens: "Weather, now, 18 lines. Small knob: line by line." and the first line, the temperature, "12.3 degrees C".
Turn the small knob: one line at a time, with its place: "Wind, NW 15 km/h, 6 of 18", "Humidity, 80 %, 8 of 18".
F1 Now, F2 5 days, F3 Warnings: each view read the same way, its name, how many lines, the first one.
In 5 days, each day starts with its name and temperatures: "Thu, 14 degrees C / 8".
F4 Update: a fresh forecast, read when it comes. F5 turns warning notices on and off.

2. Settings > Info (page 3, F2): line by line
Turn the small knob: each line, "Version, v0.3.12, 1 of 15", "Baseband, ...", "Battery, 7.7 V 100%", with the buttons (Update, Factory reset) in their place.
The same in any other Settings card with information rows.

3. Settings rows say their place
Turning through any Settings tab: each row ends with its place, "Speaker mode, off, 1 of 22".

4. Rows with two controls
Settings > Audio (F4): "Line-in, 10" and "Line-out, 10" for the two of that row, "Play, ..." and "Rec gain, 0.0" for the next.

5. Date and Time
Settings > General: "Day, 18", "Month, 11", "Year, 2026", "Hour, 20", "Minute, 15", "Second, 30".

6. Display (F3), the first row
"Screen dims after, seconds, 0 never, 30", then "Brightness, dimmed and normal, from 10 to 30".

7. VOL in an open list
Settings > General > Country: press the small knob to open the list, turn a little, press VOL.
Expect: the list closes, the country as it was is said, and Settings stays open.

8. The F-keys said
Opening any app now says its F-keys with its name: SWR Scan (APPS number 8), "SWR Scan. F1 Run, F2 Scale, Log, F3 Span, 200 kHz".
A tap of the power key ends with the F-keys of where you are: "... F-keys: F1 Run, F2 Scale, ..." in a window, and on the main screen the page shown.

9. Keys that do nothing now say so
An F-key with no button on the page: "F5, not used".
A button with no long press, held: "Run, no long press".
A long press with no action set in Settings: "No long press action set".
Mode keys while an app holds the mode (FT8, JS8 and the like): "Mode locked by the app". A/B there: "VFO locked by the app".
V/M in a window: "V M, not used here". Any other key the radio has no use for: "Not used".
Tell me any key that still does nothing in silence, and where you were.

10. SWR scan result rounded: "lowest S W R 1.2 at 14.118".

11. Accented letters
Country list: "Aland Islands" said whole (it was "land Islands"). Weather: "degrees" for the degree sign.
