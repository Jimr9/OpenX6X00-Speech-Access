OpenX6X00 speech, test 4

Only what changed since test 3. Everything in TESTING-speech-test3.txt still applies.
Write after each test: OK, silent, or wrong (and what it said).
Your WiFi has to be joined again after flashing (Settings, F5 Wireless). The SSH key for me stays on the card.


1. Dropdown lists (the fix for Country and Zone)
Settings > General: turn to Country, press the small knob to open the list, turn.
Expect: each country as you move, with its place: "Argentina, 8 of 250". Press to choose it.
Do the same on Zone: "America/Chicago (CST/CDT), 45 of 120".
Press VOL to close a list without choosing.

2. Settings tabs
Open Settings.
Expect: "Settings. Tabs on the F-keys, page 1 of 3: F2 General, F3 Display, F4 Audio, F5 Wireless. F1 more tabs", then the first row.
Press F1: "Settings tabs, page 2 of 3: F2 Bluetooth, F3 Keys, F4 Cluster, F5 TX Cal. F1 more tabs".
Press F5 (or another tab): the tab's name, then its first row, for example "Wireless. Speaker mode, off".

3. F-key pages say what is on F2 to F5
Tap GEN.
Expect: "VOL page 1. F2 Volume, 25, F3 Squelch, 0, F4 RF gain, 63, F5 TX power, 5 W" (your values).
Tap DFL. Expect: "Filter page. F1 Filter low, ..., F2 Filter high, ...".

4. Frequencies
Tune to 14.074 and stop.
Expect: "14.074" (it said "14.74" before, the same as 14.740).
On 6 meters: "50.053.500" and so on.

5. The address
Power key tap on the main screen: at the end, "WiFi address 192 dot 168 dot 5 dot 80".
Settings > Wireless: your network's row says "connected, address 192 dot 168 ...".

6. Antenna tuner
Hold ATU to start a tuning cycle (antenna or dummy load on). Nothing is said while it tunes.
Expect, when it finishes: "Tuner done, S W R 1.2" (the SWR it reached). If it fails, as before: "Tuner: no match" or "Tuner stopped".

7. Manual TUNE (the Ext tune button, for an external tuner; antenna or dummy load on)
Let it run to its limit, or press the button again to stop it.
Expect: "Tune over, 10 second limit, 5.0 watts, S W R 1.3", or "Tune stopped" with the reason and the same readings.

8. Messages during a transmission
Anything the radio reports while transmitting is now said as soon as it receives again. For example the SWR protection "TX stopped: SWR 3.1", or "TX 0 W" when no power comes out. Before, these were lost. If you see one come up, tell me whether you heard it after the over.

9. Number boxes
Settings > General > Date and Time, or a TX shift: numbers are said plain, "30" rather than "+00030", "minus" for a minus sign.
