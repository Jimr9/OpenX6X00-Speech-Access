OpenX6X00 speech, test 2

What changed since test 1:
- When the screen had gone dark or the screen saver was on, the first key press or knob turn only woke the screen and did nothing else. That is why single steps of the VOL and small knobs were silent. With speech on, that first press or turn now acts too.
- The volume is spoken wherever it changes: the main screen, the apps, and the first-start guide.
- The APPS screen and the Digital modes screen read one item at a time, with its place, for example "Cluster, DX spots, 2 of 23". They no longer read the whole list at once.
- This build keeps a speech log in the radio's memory (/tmp/voice.log). It is gone when you power off. If something is silent, do not power off: I can read the log over SSH.

Write your result after each test, as before: OK, silent, or wrong (and what it said).
